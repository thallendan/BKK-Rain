NOAA Live Walker Telemetry Factors & Bangkok Hydrological Impacts

850 hPa Trade Wind Anomaly (CPAC850)
Range: -10 to +10 m/s

Measures lower-troposphere zonal wind anomaly across the Central Pacific (175°W–140°W) at ~1.5 km altitude. Per NOAA CPC index conventions:

  • > +1.5 m/s (La Niña Signal): Strong easterly trade winds push warm ocean water westward toward Southeast Asia, amplifying monsoon rainfall in Northern/Central Thailand.
  • < -1.5 m/s (El Niño Signal): Westerly wind anomalies weaken trade winds, suppressing monsoon moisture transport and elevating drought risk.
  • -1.5 to +1.5 m/s: Neutral trade wind conditions.
Tahiti-Darwin Southern Oscillation Index (SOI)
Range: -3.0 to +3.0

Calculates standardized air pressure differences between Tahiti (East Pacific) and Darwin, Australia (West Pacific). Standardized monthly values:

  • > +0.8 (La Niña Phase): High pressure over East Pacific and low pressure over Indonesia/Thailand drives Walker cell rising branch directly over the Gulf of Thailand.
  • < -0.8 (El Niño Phase): Low pressure shifts eastward into the Central Pacific, creating atmospheric subsidence and dry conditions over Indochina.
  • -0.8 to +0.8: Neutral atmospheric pressure gradient.
Central Pacific OLR Anomaly (Outgoing Longwave)
Range: -50 to +50 W/m²

Measures thermal radiation emitted into space from the equatorial atmosphere (160°E–160°W). Anomaly values:

  • > +10 W/m² (Suppressed CPAC Cloudiness): Clear skies in Central Pacific correspond to enhanced convection and heavy monsoon cloud buildup over Thailand.
  • < -10 W/m² (Enhanced CPAC Cloudiness): Deep convective clouds shift eastward to Central Pacific, starving the Asian monsoon of moisture.
  • -10 to +10 W/m²: Normal equatorial cloud coverage.
0-300m Upper Ocean Heat Content Anomaly
Range: -4.0 to +4.0 °C

Quantifies total thermal energy stored in the upper 300 meters of the Equatorial Pacific ocean (180°W–100°W):

  • < -0.5 °C (Subsurface Cold Pool): Thermocline shallowing in East Pacific sustains La Niña conditions and prolonged wet spells in Greater Bangkok.
  • > +0.5 °C (Subsurface Warm Pool): Subsurface warm kelvin waves propagate eastward, maintaining El Niño warming and lower dam inflows.
  • -0.5 to +0.5 °C: Normal subsurface oceanic thermal state.

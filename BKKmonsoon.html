China high − Bay of Bengal low (hPa)

Regimes use the 850 hPa wind direction (from): SW 200–290° = westerly monsoon from Andaman/Bay of Bengal; NE 20–90° = China/Siberian-high surge; E 90–200° = Pacific/typhoon-fed easterlies; N = continental northerlies; WEAK <2.5 m/s. A bigger high–low pressure difference means a stronger NE surge. Data: Open-Meteo (GFS/ECMWF), GDACS, plus your data.json. Model output, not an official forecast.
